# ShowMe

Public reader for U.S. congressional STOCK Act periodic transaction reports disclosed in 2026.

Open the page: <https://irostyslav.github.io/apps/showme/>

## What is real

- 2,950 buy and sell rows filed in 2026 from [Hillscore trades.csv](https://hillscore.com/data/) (priced subset, file updated 22 Sep 2026). Not every member of Congress.
- Three additional rows from [Congress Stock Tracker](https://www.congressstock.com/trades), also described by Benzinga on 22 Sep 2026: Pete Sessions sold GOOGL and ABBV; Mitch McConnell’s spouse bought WFC. Those three are not in the Hillscore priced file.
- Later percent moves are from Hillscore outcomes.csv. They are the stock’s move, not the member’s profit. Filings do not include share counts.
- Quotes on the live preview are a delayed Yahoo chart snapshot for 18 symbols, as of 22 Sep 2026. This page’s day-mover list uses that same snapshot.
- IPO notes are from IPOScoop, Yahoo Finance, TipRanks, ValueVerge, and Bloomberg. Bamboo (BMB) is listed for 23 Sep 2026 by several calendars and marked postponed on IPOScoop.
